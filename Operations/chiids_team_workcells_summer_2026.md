5. Flag publication candidates for CUNY Academic Works pipeline.

## Media Storage Policy

Use a three-tier storage split.

1. Dropbox: primary storage for raw media (source of truth).
2. Documentary repository: curated edits, selected packages, and publication candidate bundles.
3. CHIIDS and Operations logs: metadata index, workflow status, and publication routing state.

Reference: `Operations/chiids_media_logging_workflow_summer_2026.md`.

## Camera and Photo-Capture Rig (Practical Baseline)

Use the best available camera first. If no dedicated camera is available, use a recent iPhone with a simple stabilization setup.

### Minimum Rig

1. iPhone (primary camera) with high-resolution stills enabled.
2. Tripod or fixed mount.
3. Consistent lighting (avoid mixed color temperatures when possible).
4. Neutral background area for removed artifacts when needed.
5. Scale reference card/ruler for size-sensitive items.

### Capture Settings and Process

1. Capture one context photo (in-place), then one front-on documentation photo.
2. Use consistent naming with artifact ID in filename.
3. Avoid perspective distortion for poster/document artifacts.
4. Ensure text is legible at full resolution before moving to next item.
5. Capture date, operator, and location immediately in catalog row.

## File and Naming Convention

- Inventory images: CHI-INV-xxxx_01.jpg
- Artifact images: CHI-WALL-xxxx_01.jpg
- Optional detail images: CHI-WALL-xxxx_02.jpg, CHI-WALL-xxxx_03.jpg

## Decision Authority

1. Keep in room: operationally active and needed for current work.
2. Move to foyer: display-worthy and suitable for public-facing context.
3. Archive: historically useful but not currently active.
4. Retire: duplicate/obsolete with no current operational or archival value.
